# Disclaimer

jūmyō is AI software for personal longevity research and education. It does not provide medical care, does not diagnose, treat or prescribe, and does not replace your clinician. Its outputs are generated automatically, are not reviewed by any clinician before you see them, and may be wrong. Automated checks do not remove that risk, and no health or longevity outcome is promised. Talk to your clinician before starting, stopping or changing any medication, supplement or health routine, and do not delay care because of an output. In an emergency, contact local emergency services.

**Your data.** Your records stay in a folder on your computer (`~/.jumyo`), which every Claude account used on that computer can open. What the agents read is processed by Anthropic under the terms of the Claude account in use. Evidence searches send topics, never personal data, to public services such as PubMed and openFDA. jūmyō has no server and sends nothing anywhere by itself.

For adults only.
